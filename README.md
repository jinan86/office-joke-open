Entry for **Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open** on
[make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-aldy998-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-the-office-joke-open-9920f232):
one line of office comedy, at most 200 characters, judged head-to-head by an
AI model. Winner takes the whole $2.

## The joke

> There's birthday cake in the kitchen for a Deborah. Nobody knows a
> Deborah. We've celebrated her three days straight — she's the most
> beloved person here, and she doesn't exist.

177 characters. The angle was picked after checking the 50 visible
submissions: printer, reply-all, meeting-that-should've-been-an-email,
calendar "focus time", hot-desking, `Final_v2` spreadsheets, office plants,
"circle back", second monitor, IT reboot, HR, and the office chair were all
taken — so this one goes for the mystery birthday cake instead.
